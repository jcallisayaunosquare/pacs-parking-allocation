## Direction contract

THESIS: PACS publishes your place in line, so the screen's subject is a number, not a
greeting. It refuses the dashboard-of-equal-cards arrangement every allocation product
ships: a resident does not want six tiles, they want to know where they stand and when
their turn comes. The one idea this product owns is that waiting is visible, ordered and
public, and that the order is arrived at by a stated rule rather than by discretion.

OWN-WORLD: The civic call board and the printed ticket. Ground is deep slate at full
commitment, not a tinted white; the board is a dark object in a lit room and the numbers
are the light. One luminous signal colour carries position, turn and action, and appears
nowhere decorative. Numerals are tabular and set at display scale: rank is read by size
and by position in a fixed column, never by colour alone and never by a chart. The ticket
is the second material - a pale stub with one perforated edge and a torn fibre line, the
only soft thing on any screen, carrying what is yours. Rules are hairlines; depth is by
layer and by ground value, never by a drop shadow. Tabs, radii and ornament stay off: a
call board has no rounded corners and no cards.

STORY: The resident understands that their wait is counted, that the count is the rule,
and that the board will reach them. They believe the order because they can read it and
recompute their own draw token. They register, and then they watch their row rise.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Resident - the slate board fills the viewport. Top left, small, the
period and its close date. Centre left at display scale, their own number: position if
waiting, bay code if allocated, with the accrued wait set beneath it in tabular figures.
Right half, the board itself: the ranked rows, fixed columns, the viewer's row marked,
and the NOW SERVING rule drawn across the table where the bays run out. The primary
action sits directly under the number, left, as the only filled element on the screen.
Administrator - the same board, the register in place of the single number.

FORM: Civic queue and turn system, candidate 1 of my ordered list, chosen by the user
over the dealt assignment (seed key d53d4f93, assigned index 6, the league standings
direction). Raises carried in from the declined and competitive hands: COLUMNS NEVER MOVE
(split-flap concourse) - rows are live entities ranked by time while columns hold still,
so a redraw never reflows the reader's eye. ONE SIZE DOES THE WORK (acetate tab manual) -
a single body size carries the interface, with scale spent only on the numbers that are
the subject, and depth by layer rather than shadow. ONE ADDRESSABLE SPACE (convention
catalog) - a board row and its bay on the lot plan are the same object, addressable from
either side. RANK OFF THE TYPE CHANNEL (star atlas) - position is carried by magnitude
and placement, so the type stays one size and still ranks.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review,
the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
